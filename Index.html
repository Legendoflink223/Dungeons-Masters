What is a DM

A DM or Dungeons master also reffered to as a GM or Game master is the one who Runs all the game. They use books or write and prepare a original written by them story. most of the time a DM does the planning as well as preparing for the games ahead. It is not uncommon to see many games planned out ahead of many weeks or months depending on game meet ups.

What should players bring?

These are some common items to bring to games as a player and Dungeon master.

  • A set of dice containing a D4,D6,D8,2 D10's,D12,and a D20, a D100 is also useful
  • character sheet digital or physical
  • note taking material
  • Copy of the core rule book
  • Copy of the Dungeon masters guide
  • Any visuals to help the imagination
