What is D&D?

Dungeons and Dragons is a Table Top Role Playing game. Created in 1974 by Gary Gygax and Dave Arneson, it functions as a cooperative, live-action storytelling game where players use their imaginations, character sheets, and dice rolls rather than a traditional board to navigate an adventure.

Players will sit either in person or online to have a Dungeon Master (DM) run a game by telling a story by words. Players will have to use imagination and creative use of music, sounds and sometimes visuals for the world and the people and creatures within it. PLayers will use character sheets in order to play a character of their creation in order to play in the world. At times when choices or actions are being made a DM will ask players to roll Checks, Saving throws, or Damage via Dice and Modifiers on the character sheet. The results can range from in your favor to being sent to jail for accidentally killing the king with a fork because you tripped and fell while trying to walk.
